Our Story

Reading is where it starts. Character is where it goes.

Genova is a generation of bright minds: little stars, bursts of light, wonder walking into a room. A Genova story is written for every child.

This all began with a simple worry. In the rush of modern life driven by technology, a lot of children are losing something precious: connection. To Faith, culture, history, and even people.

As an educator working closely with young readers, I saw children struggling not just to read, but to name their feelings, navigate friendships, and hold onto values in a noisy world. I've also seen firsthand how far a single story could carry a child: into confidence, into character, into conversation with the people who love them most.

So the Genova Project was created as an initiative under CUSTAR (whose mission is to teach, guide and equip young minds for the future): grounded in Faith, woven with African culture, and designed to nurture readers and spark a conversation that lasts long after the last page.

This is not just a solution to screen time, but a doorway back to strong family bonds, character development, and God-consciousness. Every story here is a small invitation to read together, feel together, and grow together.

Meet the 7 Pillars
The Genova Framework

Seven pillars. One whole child.

Every Genova story is built on one or a blend of seven pillars, so every reader is engaged through systemic learning and wholesome development.

🌍
Stories woven with culture

Nigerian and African

Language, tradition, community, and everyday life in a story. Naming ceremonies, weddings, a proverb from grandma's mouth, so your child's world is the one being celebrated.

💛
Understanding and communicating feelings, not hiding them.

Emotional Intelligence

Self-regulation, empathy, honesty and patience: the inner toolkit no toy or app can hand a child. Stories that give big feelings small, manageable words.

🧮
The classroom delivered through stories.

Subject-Based Learning

Maths, science, business, geography, culture and leadership folded into stories, so a child absorbs a subject without ever finding it difficult or boring.

⚙️
Every "why?", answered with wonder.

How Things Work

From how an airplane flies to how the internet carries a message, behind-the-scenes stories that turn why into wonder, gently simplified for young minds.

🏡
The stories they're already living.

Real-Life Inspired Scenarios

Moving house, an owambe weekend, a sibling leaving for boarding school, drawn from real family and community life, so every child sees themselves on the page.

📱
Screens aren't the enemy.

Responsible Screen Use

Grounded in real psychological and neurological research, told with a child's gentleness, helping children notice their own screen habits and choosing valuable content.

🕊️
Timeless wisdom, no sermon required.

Scripture-Rooted Life Principles

Honesty, diligence, stewardship, humility, justice, contentment, courage, forgiveness, hospitality, and wisdom, lived out in everyday scenes any child can recognize.
