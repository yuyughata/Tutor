The Genova App

Genova stories, in your child's hands, at just their level.

A safe, ad-free storybook app. Every story has a picture on every page and words that match your child's reading level. Subscribe on this website, then read on your phone or tablet.

🚫
Safe by design

No ads, ever

No third-party trackers, no ads, and nothing to buy inside the app, so a child can never buy anything by accident.

🔒
Grown-ups in charge

Parent-protected

Grown-up areas sit behind a parental gate and a 4-digit passcode only you know. Kiosk mode keeps a child inside Genova.

📴
Stories that travel

Read offline

Save stories to the device and read them on a long journey or anywhere without signal.

🔍
A new word in every story

Word Explorer

Spark stories have one new word to explore and Seeker stories have three, with a child-friendly meaning and an example.

📖
Bigger books, easy to navigate

Chapters

Longer books are split into chapters. Children see the chapter list and can jump to the one they want.

🏅
Celebrate every milestone

Badges and rewards

Milestone badges for reading, and a word garden that grows with every new word. No timers and no streaks to lose.

1

2
