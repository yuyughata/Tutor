Meet the Genova Groups

Every child reads differently.
Genova meets them where they are.

Three reading levels, each with its own stories, pace and support, so your child never feels behind.

Genova Sunrise Assisted Reader

Sunrise

For the little ones

For the little one who still needs your voice to bring the story alive. Big adventures and gentle lessons.

Genova Spark Emergent Reader

Spark

For the brave beginner

For brave beginners finding their way into reading. Carefully paced blending practice helps build confidence one word at a time, and celebrate early wins.

Genova Seeker Developing Reader

Seeker

For the confident reader

For the reader ready to go deeper. Richer stories, bigger questions, and real-life choices that stretch character, knowledge and independence all at once.
