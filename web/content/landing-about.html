Our Story

Reading is where it starts. Character is where it goes.

Genova is a generation of bright minds: little stars, bursts of light, wonder walking into a room. A Genova story is written for every child.

This all began with a simple worry. In the rush of modern life driven by technology, a lot of children are losing something precious: connection. To Faith, culture, history, and even people.

As an educator working closely with young readers, I saw children struggling not just to read, but to name their feelings, navigate friendships, and hold onto values in a noisy world. I've also seen firsthand how far a single story could carry a child: into confidence, into character, into conversation with the people who love them most.

So the Genova Project was created as an initiative under CUSTAR (whose mission is to teach, guide and equip young minds for the future): grounded in Faith, woven with African culture, and designed to nurture readers and spark a conversation that lasts long after the last page.

This is not just a solution to screen time, but a doorway back to strong family bonds, character development, and God-consciousness. Every story here is a small invitation to read together, feel together, and grow together.
